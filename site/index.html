Design

A real canvas — not a wireframe toy.

Frames, artboards, nested layers, a Sketch-grade inspector, boolean shapes, align & distribute, shared styles. Build product UIs, not just boxes.

Artboards & layers

iPhone, Android, tablet, desktop presets. A nested layer tree with lock, reorder, and rename.

Boolean shapes

Union, Subtract, Intersect, Difference — real vector paths you can keep editing.

Figma-grade inspector

Position, size, opacity, fill, stroke, effects — tweak any layer precisely.

Align & distribute

Snap, align edges, distribute evenly — the muscle memory you already have.

Shared styles & tokens

Color & text styles apply across the doc. Your design system, versioned.

Opens their files

Import Sketch, PDF & Word; paste from Figma. Edit the PDF someone sent you.

AI companion

An AI that actually draws — on your device.

Describe a screen and it builds real, editable layers. Keyless automations (table of contents, grids, nav bars, flowcharts) run offline with no model at all. Bring a local model (LM Studio / Ollama) or Apple Intelligence for conversation. No keys, no per-use bills.

Describe → it draws

“A fitness app home screen” becomes real frames and layers you can refine.

Keyless automations

Table of contents, card grids, nav bars, checklists, flowcharts — instant, offline.

MCP server + SKILL

Draften is an MCP server — drive it from Claude. A SKILL.md teaches any model to use it well.

Git-backed · for people who can't pay the subscription

Your designs are files. Your account is your git login.

No account to create, no cloud to pay for. Sign in with a free GitHub token, and your designs branch, commit, and open PRs like any other code. GitLab & more are on the way.

Install or open in browser

Grab the build for your OS, or just use the web app. No sign-up wall.

Connect a GitHub token

Create a free token with repo scope, paste it once. That's your account.

Design on a real canvas

Frames, layers, the AI companion — make real product UIs.

Commit to your repo

Save as open-format files, commit, branch, open a PR. Your history is git history.

Full walkthrough in the documentation, including creating the token step-by-step.
